## Visual identity

Palette (unchanged from the original build): Deep Burgundy #4B101B, Dark Wine
#290B12, Champagne Gold #D8B477, Warm Gold #B78B50, Warm Ivory #F5F0E8, Charcoal
#151313, Muted Rose #A77C80. Warm ivory and charcoal sections balance the burgundy;
gold is a subtle accent. The identity must feel sophisticated and established —
creativity, speed of execution, cinematic quality, premium production, innovation,
professionalism, visual storytelling — never racing/gaming/aggressive.

Design language: handcrafted agency feel — luxury fashion editorial, premium film
studios, international ad agencies, Apple's design philosophy. Editorial layouts,
asymmetrical compositions, large cinematic imagery, whitespace, fine separators,
subtle glass, controlled shadows, elegant transitions. Avoid generic AI-startup
aesthetics, excessive gradients/glow, repetitive rounded cards, unnecessary 3D.

- Hero headline: **"WE MAKE IDEAS MOVE."**

## Video & portfolio presentation

Strong visual presence per project: looping previews, quality posters, mixed
aspect ratios (landscape, 9:16 vertical, square), full-screen playback,
hover-to-play on desktop, tap-to-play on mobile, autoplay muted only when
appropriate, pause off-screen, efficient loading. Portfolio is the primary
selling point: editorial grid with title, industry, creative description, video
preview, category, optional deliverables/package label, View Project CTA. Use
existing project data only — never invent client names, partnerships,
testimonials, or results. Portfolio stays editable from the admin dashboard.

## Pricing

Packages renamed: **Starter Campaign**, **Professional Campaign**, **Bespoke
Production**. Display configured pricing; otherwise "Custom Quote". Admin can
edit names, pricing, currency, deliverables, video duration, revision limits,
delivery timelines, additional services. Never invent prices. Elegant editorial
presentation, not generic SaaS pricing cards.

## Copy

Professional, natural, concise studio copywriting; confident without exaggerated
claims. Clients provide product images, raw footage, brand assets, or references;
the studio transforms them into premium advertising creatives.

## Admin & technical

Preserve the existing admin dashboard, authentication, database, portfolio /
pricing / inquiry management, and media uploads. Brand name, tagline, logo PNG,
favicon, contact details, social links, brand colors, and SEO info are centrally
editable settings. Refine the existing build — do not rebuild from scratch, do
not replace the framework or backend. Final checks: every page swept for old
VELORA references; navbar, homepage, footer, admin, and all responsive layouts
inspected; no broken assets, navigation, or console errors.

**Final direction:** RAPID MOTION STUDIO should look like a premium international
creative production company — memorable, sophisticated, cinematic, professional —
a real creative studio built by a world-class design and development team.
